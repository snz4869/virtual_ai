# Office Interior Pixel-Art Assets

All 31 interior office sprites (`public/assets/office/*.png`) are 16-bit top-down LPC pixel art sourced from OpenGameArt.org:

- **LPC Revised: The Office** (`https://opengameart.org/content/lpc-revised-the-office`, CC-BY-SA 3.0 / OGA-BY 3.0): `desk_front`, `desk_back`, `desk_vertical`, `laptop_front`, `laptop_silver`, `laptop_right`, `laptop_back`, `water_cooler`, `coffee_maker`, `coffee_cup`, `copy_machine`, `tv_widescreen`, `tv_clean`, `sink`
- **LPC Wooden Furniture** (`https://opengameart.org/content/lpc-wooden-furniture`, CC-BY-SA 3.0 / GPL 3.0): `bookshelf`, `round_table`, `wood_stool`, `meeting_table`, `cafe_counter`
- **LPC City Inside** (`https://opengameart.org/content/lpc-city-inside`, CC-BY-SA 3.0 / GPL 3.0): `plant_palm`, `plant_ficus`
- **LPC Floors** (`https://opengameart.org/content/lpc-floors`, CC-BY-SA 3.0): `floor_wood_light`, `floor_wood_warm`, `floor_slate_tech`, `floor_tile_light`, `rug_blue`, `rug_green`, `rug_gold`
- **LPC Frama Sci-Fi Extensions** (`https://opengameart.org/content/lpc-frama-sci-fi-extensions`, CC-BY-SA 3.0 / GPL 3.0): `server_rack`, `server_screen`, `exit_door`
