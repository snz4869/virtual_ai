# Character Sprite Assets

## Office Workers & Director (LPC 16-bit 64×64, `576×256` 9×4 Walk Spritesheets)
All 20 office characters (`director.png` + 19 `npc_*.png` for Core AI Agents, Extra Agent Slots, and Ambient Office Staff) are composited in modern corporate/startup attire (suits, blazers, ties, dress shirts, office blouses, straight skirts, vests, glasses, and barista apron) from the open-source **Universal LPC Spritesheet Character Generator** (`https://github.com/sanderfrenken/Universal-LPC-Spritesheet-Character-Generator`, CC-BY-SA 3.0 / GPL 3.0).

## Village Residents (Cabbit 24×32, `72×128` 3×4 Walk Spritesheets)
All 14 village residents (`npc_villager_bagas`, `npc_villager_deni`, `npc_barista_f`, `npc_maker`, `npc_booklover`, `npc_sailor`, `npc_gardener`, `npc_painter`, `npc_athlete`, `npc_elder_rt`, `npc_botanist`, `npc_chef`, `npc_musician`, `npc_farmer`) use the **Cabbit 24×32 Character Series** on OpenGameArt.org by **Svetlana Kushnariova (Cabbit)** & **AntumDeluge (Jordan Irwin)** (CC-BY 3.0 / OGA-BY 3.0).
